Why we exist

Garba deserves better than this

Somewhere along the way, Garba got outsourced. Bigger venues, thinner meaning. Playback tracks instead of dhol. Floors so packed you couldn't complete a single round. Food courts that had never heard of a Jain counter.

Arise Entertainments exists to reverse that. We are nine friends who grew up inside these celebrations, and we run our events like people who will personally be dancing in them — because we will be.

We do Navratri, Garba and Dandiya Raas as our core, and we extend the same standard to sangeet nights, Uttarayan, Diwali melas and every Gujarati occasion worth doing properly.

What we stand for

Our Values

Tradition First

Aarti before anything else. Real taal, real dhol, real folk sequences — Sanedo, Titodo, Hinch, do-taali and traan-taali, done the way they were meant to be.

Everyone Is Welcome

All ages, all families. Alcohol-free floors, a women's help desk, clearly marked Jain and satvik food, and staff who actually help.

Capped, Not Crammed

We deliberately cap attendance. A Garba circle should be a circle — not a crowd shuffling sideways.

Honest Operations

Clear pricing, no hidden charges, licensed vendors, insured setups and complete compliance. Celebration without consequences.
