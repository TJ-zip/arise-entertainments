Who we are

We don't throw parties.
We build memories that smell of mogra & dhol.

Arise Entertainments is a Gujarati-hearted event house born out of one simple frustration — Garba nights had stopped feeling like Garba. Too much noise, too little soul.

So nine friends got together, pooled their obsession for sound, light, taal and traditional detailing, and decided to do it properly. Authentic Sanedo. Real dhol. Clean floors, safe crowds, honest food, and a stage that respects Maa Ambe before it respects the DJ.

  • Live dhol-taasha & traditional Garba orchestra
  • Curated Sanedo, Titodo, Hinch & Do-Taali / Traan-Taali rounds
  • Family-first, safety-first crowd management
  • Authentic Gujarati food stalls & chaas counters
