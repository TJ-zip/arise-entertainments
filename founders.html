How it started

Nine friends, one stubborn idea

It began the way most good Gujarati ideas do — after a mediocre Garba night, standing outside a venue, complaining. Bad sound. Overcrowded floor. A DJ who hadn't heard a real Sanedo in his life.

Instead of complaining again the next year, nine of us decided to build the night we actually wanted to attend. We pooled savings, called every vendor we knew, argued endlessly about everything from dhol placement to chaas pricing — and Arise Entertainments happened.

Our first edition is a Pre-Navratri Garba, held before Paryushan so nobody in our community has to pick between celebrating and observing. It's a small decision, but it says everything about how we intend to run this.
